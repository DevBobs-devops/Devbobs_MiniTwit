\section{Use of Generative AI}\label{use-of-generative-ai}

Generative AI has been used to help solve issues with debugging errors
and helped with parts of the coding, that was unfamiliar to us. The used Ais are ChatGPT and Claude AI.
They have been co-authored when they have been used, often with a small
message, explaining what they helped with. They have helped us get a better
understanding of how our code works and potential errors that can occur.
Obviously, there is a possibility of loss of learning, as
AI speeds up the problemsolving process, sometimes highlighting issues
we had not observed yet.
